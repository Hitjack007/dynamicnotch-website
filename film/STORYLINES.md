# DynamicNotch hero film — storylines

Source: `film/source/screen-recording.mp4` (64 s screen recording, 1080p60). Every shot is the real app, cropped and punched in; nothing is redrawn.
Output: 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 20–25 s, silent (it autoplays muted on the site) and built to loop.

## A. The notch was doing nothing (recommended)

**Device:** life before and after. **Form:** one continuous take, locked on the notch.

The camera sits on the top edge of a MacBook screen, on the black notch in an empty menu bar. Nothing happens, long enough to notice. Then the notch opens into Now Playing, and without a cut it becomes the volume HUD, the AI usage ring and the file shelf. Each is named by one large word, until the camera pulls back to show the whole desktop.

- **First 2 s:** a tight crop of the closed notch and an empty menu bar on the real swirl wallpaper, with the line "Your notch does nothing" setting in under it.
- **Beats:** idle notch → it opens to Now Playing (title: DynamicNotch) → volume HUD → AI usage ring → shelf → pull back to the full desktop → end card (app icon, "Download for macOS") → the notch closes, back to frame 0 for the loop.
- **Look:** the recording's own black and silver swirl wallpaper, Geist type in white, the app icon's blue used once, on the end card.
- **Sound:** none (muted autoplay).

## B. One song, all the way through

**Device:** one object across apps. **Form:** an object-to-object chain.

A song carries the whole film. Its album art is clicked in Music and flies up into the notch. The volume rises, so the HUD appears. The song keeps playing while you check your AI usage and drop a file on the shelf, and each step is opened by the thing before it.

- **First 2 s:** a punch-in on the album art in Music; the cursor clicks it and the art lifts out of the window towards the top of the screen.
- **Beats:** album art clicked → it lands in the notch as Now Playing → volume up, HUD → AI usage → file onto the shelf → the song card shrinks into the app icon → end card.
- **Look:** shifts from the bright Music window to the dark desktop; Geist type; the album art's colours carry it, then the app blue on the end card.
- **Sound:** none (muted autoplay).

## C. Words that open (the bold one)

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat, using match cuts.

Giant words fill the frame one at a time: MUSIC, VOLUME, USAGE, FILES. Each word is a window: the real notch footage plays inside its letters. The O in NOTCH is the notch itself, and it opens.

- **First 2 s:** "NOTCH" fills the frame in heavy white type on black; the O is a notch-shaped cut-out that opens to show Now Playing inside it.
- **Beats:** NOTCH opens → MUSIC (footage inside the letters) → VOLUME → USAGE → FILES → the letters collapse into the notch shape → it becomes the app icon → end card.
- **Look:** pure black and white type fields with real footage only inside the letters; the app blue on one word.
- **Sound:** none (muted autoplay).
